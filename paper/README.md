# Paper source

`physics_gnn_symbolic_discovery.tex` is the submission draft aligned with the archived v9 training history and the restored 400-candidate audit. The large data arrays and checkpoints are attached to the versioned release listed in `../ARTIFACTS.md`.

The manuscript deliberately avoids presenting rediscovered corollaries as new physics. Its contribution is an auditable, closed-world benchmark and the evidence required to evaluate its claims.
